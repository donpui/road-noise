About the dB reading

A browser cannot access the iPhone microphone's factory calibration. RoadNoise converts the digital signal to an estimated SPL using a calibration offset. Compare it with a trusted sound meter and adjust the offset for better accuracy.

CALIBRATION

Sound level offset

Place this phone beside a trusted sound meter in a steady sound field. Adjust the offset until both readings match.
